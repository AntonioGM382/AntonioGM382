## Hey, I'm Toño 👋

Technical PM by mindset, developer by craft — a **Yeah Developer** who moves comfortably between product decisions, architecture, and code.

I connect the dots from **“why are we building this?”** to **“it’s shipped.”** I enjoy turning ambiguous problems into focused plans, aligning people around outcomes, and getting hands-on when the best next step is to build.

```text
[ PRODUCT ] ─── [ PEOPLE ] ─── [ ENGINEERING ]
      \____________ ship value ____________/
```

- 🧭 I translate product goals into clear technical direction.
- 🛠️ I build across modern frontend, backend, and cloud infrastructure.
- ⚡ I like pragmatic architecture, tight feedback loops, and useful software.
- 🌱 Always learning, iterating, and shipping.
